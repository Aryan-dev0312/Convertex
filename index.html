How ConvertX Works in 3 Steps

No software installation, no account creation, and completely safe.

1

Upload Your Files

Select or drag and drop your PDFs or images directly into the browser. Your files stay strictly in local memory and are never uploaded to any server.

Fine-tune settings like quality, page order, rotations, watermark text, or dimensions. ConvertX processes everything client-side with WebAssembly and Canvas.

Privacy First

Why Client-Side Processing Matters

Traditional online converters require you to upload confidential documents, tax forms, photos, and IDs to an unknown remote server. That poses serious privacy and security risks.

ConvertX changes the game: By utilizing modern Web APIs (WebAssembly, HTML5 Canvas, Blob URLs, and FileReader), all cryptographic calculations and rendering occur locally on your machine.

Zero server logs, zero data breaches
Works even on poor or offline networks
No file size limits or paywalls

Engineered With Open Web Tech

  • pdf-lib: Merge, split, rotate, watermark & page-numbering engine.
  • pdf.js (Mozilla): High-fidelity PDF rendering and thumbnail canvas generator.
  • jsPDF: In-browser vector document compilation from text and graphics.
  • browser-image-compression: Multi-threaded canvas image optimization.
  • JSZip: Client-side compressed archive packaging.
  • Pure Vanilla JS: Zero frameworks, fast load times, and clean architecture.
